Common questions

Does MeetQyl join my call as a bot?

No. MeetQyl records your microphone and the meeting's audio on your Mac. Nobody in the call sees a recorder join. Tell the people in the meeting that you are recording: laws differ by place.

How do I start and stop recording quickly?

Press ⌥⌘R in any app, use the menu bar control, or use the small floating recorder, which you can drag anywhere.

Can MeetQyl start recording when a call begins?

If you turn it on in Settings › Recording › Automation, MeetQyl notices when a meeting app starts using the microphone and asks whether to record. It only reads which app is using the microphone. It records without a click only for an app you set to Always record.

Where are my recordings?

On your Mac, in MeetQyl's own folder, or in the folder you chose in Settings › Storage, such as an external drive. Readable Markdown copies of your transcripts are saved there too. Deleting a meeting deletes its audio, transcript and notes.

Why is a meeting's audio gone after a week?

To protect your privacy, MeetQyl removes a meeting's audio 7 days after the meeting once its transcript is ready. The transcript, notes and tasks stay. Choose another period or Never in Settings, or turn on Keep audio for a single meeting.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no MeetQyl account. Cloud AI is optional and off by default; if you want it, you connect your own account with a supported AI provider in Settings › AI.

Is there an iPhone app?

Not yet. MeetQyl for iPhone is coming, but it is not available today. MeetQyl is currently for Mac only.

Troubleshooting

macOS asks for microphone and system audio access

MeetQyl needs both to record you and the other people in the call. Allow them when asked, or later in System Settings › Privacy & Security. MeetQyl never records the picture on your screen.

The first transcription wants to download something

Transcription runs on your Mac with a speech model of a few hundred MB. MeetQyl asks you to confirm before it downloads it once from Hugging Face. If you're offline, your recording is kept, and you can try the download again later. Installed models keep working offline.

Notes or Ask MeetQyl are not available

They use Apple Intelligence on your Mac. Turn it on in System Settings › Apple Intelligence & Siri, or connect your own cloud AI account in MeetQyl › Settings › AI.

MeetQyl can't record to my storage folder

If the folder you chose in Settings › Storage isn't available, for example because an external drive is disconnected, MeetQyl won't record somewhere else without telling you. Reconnect the drive or choose another folder in Settings › Storage.

Still stuck?

Choose Help › Export Diagnostics… and send the file to ntwrkplus@gmail.com with a short description of what happened.
