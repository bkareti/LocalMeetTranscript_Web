Privacy Policy

MeetQyl records and transcribes meetings on your Mac. It is built so that your meetings stay with you. This policy covers MeetQyl for Mac.

What MeetQyl stores

  • Recordings, transcripts, notes and tasks are stored on your Mac, in MeetQyl's own folder or in a folder you choose. They are not sent to us, and we do not run servers that receive them. MeetQyl does not sync them through iCloud.
  • Settings are stored on your Mac.
  • API keys and sign-ins for AI providers you connect are stored in your Mac's Keychain.
  • A list of what was sent to cloud AI is kept on your Mac and shown in each meeting's Activity tab.
  • Remembered voices (optional). Remember voices is off by default. If you turn it on and choose to remember a speaker, MeetQyl keeps a numeric summary of how that person sounds, linked to the name you gave, on your Mac. In some places this counts as biometric data. Only remember the voices of people who agreed to it.

What leaves your Mac, and only when you choose

  • Speech and speaker models. Before the first transcription, and when you choose another model or turn on speaker separation, MeetQyl asks you to confirm a download from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). Hugging Face receives an ordinary download request, which includes your IP address. No recording or transcript is sent. The models are data files stored on your Mac.
  • Cloud AI (optional, off by default). If you connect an AI provider (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or Microsoft 365 Copilot) with your own account or key, MeetQyl sends meeting text to that provider when you press Send after seeing the text, or after each transcription, without a preview, only if you allowed automatic analysis for that provider. The text can include names, such as attendees from your calendar, and anything said in the meeting. Before sending, MeetQyl replaces some private details, such as email addresses, phone numbers and card or bank numbers; this is best effort. Audio is never sent. That provider's own terms and privacy policy apply, including how long it keeps the text.
  • Microsoft 365 sign-in (optional). Connecting Microsoft 365 Copilot opens your organisation's Microsoft sign-in page, which asks for the Microsoft Graph permissions that Microsoft requires. MeetQyl uses the sign-in only to send the text described above to Copilot.
  • Calendar (optional). If you turn on calendar suggestions, MeetQyl reads your calendar on your Mac to suggest names for your meetings. When suggestions are off, MeetQyl does not read your calendar. Calendar data is not sent anywhere on its own; a meeting's title or attendee names can be part of text you send to a cloud AI provider.
  • Call detection (optional). If you turn on automation, MeetQyl notices when a meeting app starts using the microphone. It only reads which app is using the microphone.
  • Files you export. Markdown exports and diagnostics files are saved or shared only where you choose.

What we never do

  • No analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • No account with us is needed.
  • We never receive your meetings, and we do not sell your data. The only sharing is the cloud AI sending you choose, described above.

Your choices

  • Delete a meeting in MeetQyl to remove its audio, transcript and notes from your Mac.
  • By default, MeetQyl removes a meeting's audio 7 days after the meeting once its transcript is ready. You can choose another period or Never in Settings, and keep the audio of a single meeting.
  • Remove an AI provider's key or sign-in, or turn off automatic analysis, in Settings › AI.
  • Delete one remembered voice or all of them in Settings › Speakers.
  • Turn calendar suggestions and call detection on or off at any time in Settings.

Recording other people

Laws on recording conversations differ by place. MeetQyl reminds you to tell participants before you record. You are responsible for getting the consent your law requires.

Children

We, the makers of MeetQyl, receive no personal data from anyone, including children.
